# Privacy-notice factual inputs

Not a published Privacy Policy. REQUIRED BEFORE LAUNCH: legal entity, registered/contact address, monitored privacy email, approved lawful bases, retention schedule, transfer mechanisms, authority/complaint details and DPO/representative if required. None is invented here.

The application processes the categories in privacy-data-inventory.md and activities in records-of-processing.md. Users supply account/profile/content/preferences; native location is optional; public event websites supply event facts; payment/OAuth services return identity/subscription facts. Hosting regions and subprocessors remain unconfirmed.

Public profile is currently on by default; general-message opt-in is off and collections default private. Public avatars use public media URLs even for otherwise-private accounts. Explain this honestly and obtain a product decision on defaults before launch.

Messages are server-side encrypted at rest, NOT E2EE. BACity servers can decrypt for authorized delivery and narrow reporting workflows. Metadata remains readable to DB operators. Account deletion currently removes both message directions; the message/recipient retention policy needs approval.

Normal recommendations use interest/category/tag matching, saved-category affinity, followed organizers/venues/neighborhoods/categories/people, timing, bounded unique popularity, quality/freshness and optional proximity; diversity suppresses duplicates/repetition. Planners are ephemeral. No evidence of credit/employment/other significant-effect adjudication; counsel decides legal profiling classifications.

Foreground location is coarsened/request-scoped and not intentionally stored as GPS history. Area Watch centers are intentionally saved until separately deleted. Tile hosts and proxy tracing need deployment review. OS permission is not a declaration of legal consent basis.

Users can edit profile/preferences, turn location off, manage watches, export scoped JSON, request human review and request account deletion. Privacy request types include access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection and other; no automatic legal entitlement to every requested action. Contact for non-account/locked-account requests must be configured and monitored.

Existing erasure limitations: active Play purchases can block deletion; Stripe cancellation requires safe provider reconciliation. Financial/tombstone/moderation/public contribution records and backups may remain. Approved/rejected contribution content may still identify a person even after account fields are anonymized. Do not call this complete anonymization.

No marketing newsletter, external tracking SDK or push implementation found. Organization sponsored promotion and first-party organizer analytics exist; do not claim no commercial placement or no analytics whatsoever.

Public metadata /privacy/information exposes only configured HTTPS document URLs, version strings and contact. Version changes do not imply or require consent/acceptance. New privacy controls are not fake lawyer-approved policy pages.
