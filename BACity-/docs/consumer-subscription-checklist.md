# Consumer subscription engineering review

[BACity+](consumer-stripe-billing.md) uses server-authoritative entitlements from Stripe web and Google Play Android; Apple purchases absent. Preserve provider contracts and free participant exception. [Digital Content Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/770/oj/eng) and [Consumer Rights Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32011L0083) require qualified national/application review, not fabricated terms.

| Disclosure/control | Existing fact | Required action |
|---|---|---|
| Trader identity/contact | Not configured/approved | OWNER DECISION REQUIRED launch blocker |
| Features/functionality | Plus paywall lists actual premium tools; free features stay free | Owner approve factual description/interoperability/support |
| Price/interval/trial | Provider product/offer configuration authoritative | STORE/PROVIDER CONFIG REQUIRED verify actual pre-purchase displayed terms; no invented prices |
| Renewal/cancellation | Provider managed; cancellation semantics preserve access through verified eligible period | Review auto-renewal and cancellation disclosure with actual offer |
| Google Play management | Existing subscription-management channel | Verify configured product/package link; BACity cannot promise direct Play cancellation |
| Stripe management | Hosted Portal + safe server reconciliation | Configure allowed portal cancellation behavior |
| Withdrawal/refund/remedies | Not conclusively defined | LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED; no automatic policy invented |
| Confirmations/receipts | Provider delivery path, no contractual evidence in repository | Verify receipt and durable required information provider/owner |
| Account deletion | Active Play subscription currently blocks until paid period ends; Stripe requires safe cancellation/reconciliation | Explicit launch blocker: owner/counsel decide a safe solution, no silent entitlement/ownership change here |
| Complaints/privacy | New privacy requests; public contact unconfigured | Configure monitored support/legal contact; privacy workflow is not complete consumer complaint adjudication |

B2B organization Stripe data is separate: organization ownership and invoices must survive one member's removal when appropriate. Consumer rights must not mechanically overwrite organization contracts. Store privacy/consumer terms remain store/operator review actions, not completed legal work.
