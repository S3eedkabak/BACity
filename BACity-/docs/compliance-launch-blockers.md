## Concrete limitations / rollout

Active Google Play subscriptions currently prevent account deletion until the paid period ends; erasure/store readiness is not complete. Retained approved/rejected contribution text and audit UUIDs are not proven anonymous. Lawful bases, public defaults, age policy and retention remain unapproved. Non-account rights/contact and potentially non-account DSA notice handling are not operational while contact/classification are unresolved. No marketing engine, tracking SDK or Apple IAP is invented. Critical tar/framework advisories remain recorded in security documentation.

Before release: migrate to 0014; inject keys and offline-backfill legacy private text with writers stopped; staff monitored privacy/contact channels; approve and publish URLs/versions; implement operational backup/deletion reconciliation; sign/review relevant processor terms/transfers; verify provider purchase/cancellation disclosures, release permissions/store declarations and accessibility. New controls are not production deployment proof.
